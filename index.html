Your spectrum, live

Connect your USB2000 or USB2000+ directly to this browser. No Python driver is used.

Fixed scales make pairwise comparisons easier. Clipped counts remain clipped when the axis changes.
1 · Capture a dark

Block the fiber entrance completely without moving the setup. Keep the integration time fixed.

2 · Capture the white reference

Uncover the fiber and view illuminated white paper. Keep the lamp, fiber, and integration time fixed, then replace the paper view with your sample.

Relative reflectance = 100 × (sample − dark) / (reference − dark). Values can exceed 100%. Weak-reference and saturated pixels are omitted. Changing integration time clears both captures; repeat them whenever the illumination or geometry changes.

CSV includes wavelength, raw counts, dark, reference, reflectance, serial number, time, and exposure. Data stays in this browser until you save it; there is no automatic backup. Pause before saving to keep the CSV and plot on the same frame.

Connection details
Use Chrome or Edge. On Windows, a compatible WinUSB driver association may need one-time setup. Close other spectrometer apps before connecting. Supports USB2000 and USB2000+; the model is detected automatically.
